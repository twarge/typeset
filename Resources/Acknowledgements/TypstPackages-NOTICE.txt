document renders without a network connection. Each is included unmodified, as
published at https://packages.typst.org, and remains under its own license. The
complete license text of each package ships inside the package itself and can
be read in Typeset under Settings > Typst Packages.

The exact files of every package are also published at
https://github.com/typst/packages/tree/main/packages/preview/<name>/<version>

Replacing a package

You may modify any of these packages and use your modified version in Typeset.
Open Settings > Typst Packages, choose the package, and choose Replace with
Folder to select a folder holding your version. Typeset then uses your copy in
place of the included one; Revert returns to the original. Export gives you the
files exactly as included. Nothing in Typeset's own license terms restricts
modifying these packages or reverse engineering them to debug such
modifications.
